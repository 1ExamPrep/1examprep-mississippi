Which Mississippi exam is it for?

The Mississippi Residential Electrical license covers electrical work in homes under the state residential license. In Mississippi, a residential license is needed to build new homes valued over $50,000, and a residential license is needed for remodeling or roofing work over $10,000. PSI gives the Residential Electrical trade exam open book; the question count and time limit are in the current PSI Mississippi candidate bulletin, and the passing score on MSBOC trade exams is 70%.

How should you study with it?

Skim every highlighted passage once, then drill lookups. On an open-book exam with roughly 2 to 3 minutes a question, fast tab use matters more than memorizing. Pair it with the Law and Business Management prep, since every applicant sits that 50-question exam too.

Can I bring these books into the PSI exam?

MSBOC exams are open book, but only the references named in the PSI bulletin are allowed. Highlighting and permanent tabs are generally allowed; loose notes are not.

Every applicant also passes the Mississippi Law and Business Management exam: 50 questions, a 70% passing score and 2 hours, taken open book at PSI.
