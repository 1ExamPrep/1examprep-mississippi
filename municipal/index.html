Who needs the Mississippi Municipal and Public Works license?

The Municipal and Public Works classification covers water, sewer and storm drainage, streets and other public utility construction for towns and counties. In Mississippi, a commercial certificate of responsibility from the Mississippi State Board of Contractors is needed for commercial jobs over $50,000, including labor and materials. Most buyers are utility and public works contractors. The license goes to a business, and its qualifying party is the person who sits for the exams.

What is on the Municipal and Public Works exam?

The Municipal and Public Works exam at PSI has 60 questions and needs 70% to pass, with 120 minutes allowed. You may bring the approved books. MSBOC lets candidates use only the references named in the PSI bulletin, which is why every book package here matches that list.

Every applicant also passes the Mississippi Law and Business Management exam: 50 questions, a 70% passing score and 2 hours, taken open book at PSI. Reciprocity can waive a trade exam, but it does not waive Law and Business.
