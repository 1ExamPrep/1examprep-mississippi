Mississippi contractor exam FAQ

Mississippi contractors need a state license for commercial jobs over $50,000 and for remodeling over $10,000, and they test at PSI on 2 open-book exams with a 70% passing score. The answers below cover limits, scheduling, books and the Law and Business exam.

Key takeaways

  • Commercial jobs and new homes over $50,000 need an MSBOC license; remodeling or roofing needs one over $10,000.
  • PSI gives every exam open book, and you need 70% to pass.
  • Law and Business Management is 50 questions in 2 hours, required of every applicant.
  • Cities and counties, not the state, license journeyman electricians and plumbers.

Licenses and dollar limits

Who needs a Mississippi contractor license?

Commercial jobs over $50,000, new homes over $50,000, and residential remodeling or roofing over $10,000 all need an MSBOC license. Fire sprinkler work needs one at $5,000 on public jobs and $10,000 on private jobs.

Who gives the Mississippi contractor exams?

PSI gives every MSBOC exam, and MSBOC says all of them are open book.

What is the Law and Business Management exam?

Every applicant also passes the Mississippi Law and Business Management exam: 50 questions, a 70% passing score and 2 hours, taken open book at PSI. It is built on the Mississippi edition of the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management.

Can I take the exam before I apply?

No. You apply to MSBOC first, and PSI emails a scheduling notice only after the board confirms you are eligible.

How long does it take to get a Mississippi contractor license?

Most applicants take 6 to 10 weeks from application to license, depending on board review, PSI seat availability and study time.

Exams and scheduling

Do I need experience to get licensed?

MSBOC reviews each application against the classification you request. Check the current application on msboc.us for the experience and references it asks for.

Do residential and commercial applicants take the same business exam?

Yes. Every applicant also passes the Mississippi Law and Business Management exam: 50 questions, a 70% passing score and 2 hours, taken open book at PSI.

Is the NASCLA exam accepted in Mississippi?

Yes. MSBOC lists the NASCLA Commercial General Building and electrical exams as options for contractors who work in more than one state. Law and Business is still required.

Does Mississippi license journeyman electricians or plumbers?

Mississippi has no statewide journeyman or master license, so individual electricians and plumbers test with the city or county that issues the card. The study guides here cover those local tests.

Do I need a license for a $40,000 commercial job?

Not from the state, since the commercial limit is $50,000. Local permits and business licenses still apply.

How should I study for an open-book exam?

Open-book exams reward speed with the books more than memory. An 80-question exam in 180 minutes leaves a little over 2 minutes a question, so the goal is to know where every answer lives.

  1. Tab every approved book the day it arrives, using pre-printed tabs if you have them.
  2. Read each table of contents twice and note which book covers which topic.
  3. Take at least 2 full timed practice exams with only the approved books open.
  4. Look up every missed question and mark the page, so the next lookup takes 1 flip.
