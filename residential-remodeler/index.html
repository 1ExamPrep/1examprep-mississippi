Who needs the Mississippi Residential Remodeler license?

The Residential Remodeler classification covers additions, repairs and renovation of existing homes; a residential license is needed for remodeling or roofing work over $10,000. In Mississippi, a residential license is needed to build new homes valued over $50,000, and a residential license is needed for remodeling or roofing work over $10,000. Most buyers are remodelers and home improvement contractors. The license goes to a business, and its qualifying party is the person who sits for the exams.

What is on the Residential Remodeler exam?

The Residential Remodeler exam at PSI has 50 questions and needs 70% to pass, with 150 minutes allowed. You may bring the approved books. MSBOC lets candidates use only the references named in the PSI bulletin, which is why every book package here matches that list.

Yes. MSBOC says every exam is given by PSI and every exam is open book, so tabbed and highlighted books save real time.

Do I also take the Law and Business Management exam?

Every applicant also passes the Mississippi Law and Business Management exam: 50 questions, a 70% passing score and 2 hours, taken open book at PSI. Reciprocity can waive a trade exam, but it does not waive Law and Business.
