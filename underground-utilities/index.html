Who needs the Mississippi Underground Utilities license?

The Underground Utilities classification covers pipe, conduit and cable installed below grade, plus excavation near existing utility lines under the state excavation law. In Mississippi, a commercial certificate of responsibility from the Mississippi State Board of Contractors is needed for commercial jobs over $50,000, including labor and materials. Most buyers are pipeline, water line and directional boring contractors. The license goes to a business, and its qualifying party is the person who sits for the exams.

What is on the Underground Utilities exam?

PSI gives the Underground Utilities trade exam open book; the question count and time limit are in the current PSI Mississippi candidate bulletin, and the passing score on MSBOC trade exams is 70%. MSBOC lets candidates use only the references named in the PSI bulletin, which is why every book package here matches that list.

How many questions are on the Underground Utilities exam?

Is the Mississippi contractor exam open book?

Yes. MSBOC says every exam is given by PSI and every exam is open book, so tabbed and highlighted books save real time.

Do I also take the Law and Business Management exam?

Every applicant also passes the Mississippi Law and Business Management exam: 50 questions, a 70% passing score and 2 hours, taken open book at PSI. Reciprocity can waive a trade exam, but it does not waive Law and Business.

How do I schedule the exam?
