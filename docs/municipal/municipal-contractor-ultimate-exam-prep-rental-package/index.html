Which Mississippi exam is it for?

The Mississippi Municipal and Public Works license covers water, sewer and storm drainage, streets and other public utility construction for towns and counties. In Mississippi, a commercial certificate of responsibility from the Mississippi State Board of Contractors is needed for commercial jobs over $50,000, including labor and materials. The Municipal and Public Works exam at PSI has 60 questions and needs 70% to pass, with 120 minutes allowed. You may bring the approved books.
