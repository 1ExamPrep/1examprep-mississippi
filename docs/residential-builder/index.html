Who needs the Mississippi Residential Builder license?

The Residential Builder classification covers new home construction; a residential license is needed to build new homes valued over $50,000. In Mississippi, a residential license is needed to build new homes valued over $50,000, and a residential license is needed for remodeling or roofing work over $10,000. Most buyers are home builders in every county from DeSoto to Harrison. The license goes to a business, and its qualifying party is the person who sits for the exams.

What is on the Residential Builder exam?

The Residential Builder exam at PSI has 80 questions and needs 70% to pass, with 180 minutes allowed. You may bring the approved books. MSBOC lets candidates use only the references named in the PSI bulletin, which is why every book package here matches that list.

Do I also take the Law and Business Management exam?

Every applicant also passes the Mississippi Law and Business Management exam: 50 questions, a 70% passing score and 2 hours, taken open book at PSI. Reciprocity can waive a trade exam, but it does not waive Law and Business.
