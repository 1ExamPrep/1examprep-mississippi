Who needs the Mississippi Concrete license?

The Concrete classification covers foundations, slabs, formwork, reinforcing and concrete cutting on commercial jobs. In Mississippi, a commercial certificate of responsibility from the Mississippi State Board of Contractors is needed for commercial jobs over $50,000, including labor and materials. Most buyers are concrete and foundation contractors. The license goes to a business, and its qualifying party is the person who sits for the exams.

What is on the Concrete exam?

The Concrete exam at PSI has 50 questions and needs 70% to pass, with 120 minutes allowed. You may bring the approved books. MSBOC lets candidates use only the references named in the PSI bulletin, which is why every book package here matches that list.

Is the Mississippi contractor exam open book?

Yes. MSBOC says every exam is given by PSI and every exam is open book, so tabbed and highlighted books save real time.

Do I also take the Law and Business Management exam?

Every applicant also passes the Mississippi Law and Business Management exam: 50 questions, a 70% passing score and 2 hours, taken open book at PSI. Reciprocity can waive a trade exam, but it does not waive Law and Business.
