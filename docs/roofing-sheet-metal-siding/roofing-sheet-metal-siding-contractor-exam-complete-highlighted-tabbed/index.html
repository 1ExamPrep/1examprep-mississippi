Which Mississippi exam is it for?

The Mississippi Roofing, Sheet Metal and Siding license covers commercial roofing systems, flashing, sheet metal and siding. In Mississippi, a commercial certificate of responsibility from the Mississippi State Board of Contractors is needed for commercial jobs over $50,000, including labor and materials. The Roofing, Sheet Metal and Siding exam at PSI has 60 questions and needs 70% to pass, with 120 minutes allowed. You may bring the approved books.
