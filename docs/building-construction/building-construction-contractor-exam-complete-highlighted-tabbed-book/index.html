Which Mississippi exam is it for?

The Mississippi Building Construction license covers commercial buildings of any size, along with the concrete, masonry, carpentry, roofing and steel work that goes into them. In Mississippi, a commercial certificate of responsibility from the Mississippi State Board of Contractors is needed for commercial jobs over $50,000, including labor and materials. The Building Construction exam at PSI has 80 questions and needs 70% to pass, with 180 minutes allowed. You may bring the approved books.

How should you study with it?
