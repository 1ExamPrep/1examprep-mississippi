How do you get a Mississippi contractor license?

Getting licensed in Mississippi takes 5 steps and usually 6 to 10 weeks. The board reviews the application before PSI lets you test, so file early and study while you wait. The full walkthrough is on the licensing guide.

  1. 1

    Pick your classification

    Match your work to 1 of the 50 license types on this site and check its dollar limit.

  2. 2

    Form the business

    Register the company with the Mississippi Secretary of State and line up insurance.

  3. 3

    Apply to MSBOC

    Send the application. Commercial applicants include financial statements; the board checks eligibility.

  4. 4

    Schedule at PSI

    PSI emails a notice after approval. Book the trade exam and Law and Business together.

  5. 5

    Pass and get licensed

    Score 70% on each exam with your tabbed books, and the board issues the license.

Why do tabbed books matter on an open-book exam?

An 80-question exam in 180 minutes leaves about 2 minutes a question. Candidates who fail usually know the material but lose time hunting through 10 or more reference books. Highlighted and tabbed sets put the answer 1 flip away, and the online courses drill the same lookups until they are automatic.

Frequently asked questions

Who needs a Mississippi contractor license?

Commercial jobs over $50,000, new homes over $50,000, and residential remodeling or roofing over $10,000 all need an MSBOC license. Fire sprinkler work needs one at $5,000 on public jobs and $10,000 on private jobs.

Who gives the Mississippi contractor exams?

PSI gives every MSBOC exam, and MSBOC says all of them are open book.

What is the Law and Business Management exam?

Every applicant also passes the Mississippi Law and Business Management exam: 50 questions, a 70% passing score and 2 hours, taken open book at PSI. It is built on the Mississippi edition of the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management.

Can I take the exam before I apply?

No. You apply to MSBOC first, and PSI emails a scheduling notice only after the board confirms you are eligible.

Does Mississippi license journeyman electricians or plumbers?

Mississippi has no statewide journeyman or master license, so individual electricians and plumbers test with the city or county that issues the card. The study guides here cover those local tests.

Is the NASCLA exam accepted in Mississippi?

Yes. MSBOC lists the NASCLA Commercial General Building and electrical exams as options for contractors who work in more than one state. Law and Business is still required.

What score do I need to pass?

70% on the Law and Business exam and on the MSBOC trade exams covered here.
