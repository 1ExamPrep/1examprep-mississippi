Which Mississippi exam is it for?

The Mississippi Concrete license covers foundations, slabs, formwork, reinforcing and concrete cutting on commercial jobs. In Mississippi, a commercial certificate of responsibility from the Mississippi State Board of Contractors is needed for commercial jobs over $50,000, including labor and materials. The Concrete exam at PSI has 50 questions and needs 70% to pass, with 120 minutes allowed. You may bring the approved books.

Rentals run for a set number of months, so book the exam first and study backward from the date. Aim for 1 book or chapter group a week and 2 timed practice runs. Pair it with the Law and Business Management prep, since every applicant sits that 50-question exam too.
