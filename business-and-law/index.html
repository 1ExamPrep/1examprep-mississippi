Who needs the Mississippi Law and Business Management license?

The Law and Business Management classification is the exam every Mississippi applicant takes, commercial or residential, built on the Mississippi edition of the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management. In Mississippi, a commercial certificate of responsibility from the Mississippi State Board of Contractors is needed for commercial jobs over $50,000, including labor and materials, and a residential license is needed for remodeling or roofing work over $10,000. Most buyers are every qualifying party, whatever the trade. The license goes to a business, and its qualifying party is the person who sits for the exams.

What is on the Law and Business Management exam?

The Law and Business Management exam at PSI has 50 questions and needs 70% to pass, with 120 minutes allowed. You may bring the approved books. MSBOC lets candidates use only the references named in the PSI bulletin, which is why every book package here matches that list.

Frequently asked questions

Who needs the Mississippi Law and Business Management license?

The Law and Business Management classification is the exam every Mississippi applicant takes, commercial or residential, built on the Mississippi edition of the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management. In Mississippi, a commercial certificate of responsibility from the Mississippi State Board of Contractors is needed for commercial jobs over $50,000, including labor and materials, and a residential license is needed for remodeling or roofing work over $10,000.

How many questions are on the Law and Business Management exam?

The Law and Business Management exam at PSI has 50 questions and needs 70% to pass, with 120 minutes allowed. You may bring the approved books.

Is the Mississippi contractor exam open book?

Yes. MSBOC says every exam is given by PSI and every exam is open book, so tabbed and highlighted books save real time.

Do I also take the Law and Business Management exam?

Every applicant also passes the Mississippi Law and Business Management exam: 50 questions, a 70% passing score and 2 hours, taken open book at PSI. Reciprocity can waive a trade exam, but it does not waive Law and Business.

How do I schedule the exam?

Apply to MSBOC first. When the board confirms you are eligible, PSI emails a notice, and only then can you book a seat.
