Who needs the Mississippi NASCLA Commercial General Building license?

The NASCLA Commercial General Building classification uses the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors, which PSI gives in Mississippi and several other states accept. In Mississippi, a commercial certificate of responsibility from the Mississippi State Board of Contractors is needed for commercial jobs over $50,000, including labor and materials. Most buyers are commercial builders who plan to work in more than one state. The license goes to a business, and its qualifying party is the person who sits for the exams.

What is on the NASCLA Commercial General Building exam?

The NASCLA Commercial General Building exam at PSI has 115 questions and needs 70% to pass, with 300 minutes allowed. You may bring the approved books. MSBOC lets candidates use only the references named in the PSI bulletin, which is why every book package here matches that list.

Every applicant also passes the Mississippi Law and Business Management exam: 50 questions, a 70% passing score and 2 hours, taken open book at PSI. Reciprocity can waive a trade exam, but it does not waive Law and Business.
