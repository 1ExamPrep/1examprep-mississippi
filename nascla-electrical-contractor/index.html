Who needs the Mississippi NASCLA Electrical Contractor license?

The NASCLA Electrical Contractor classification uses the NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Electrical Contractors, which MSBOC lists as an option for contractors working in more than one state. In Mississippi, a commercial certificate of responsibility from the Mississippi State Board of Contractors is needed for commercial jobs over $50,000, including labor and materials. Most buyers are electrical contractors licensed or planning to license in several states. The license goes to a business, and its qualifying party is the person who sits for the exams.

What is on the NASCLA Electrical Contractor exam?

PSI gives the NASCLA Electrical Contractor trade exam open book; the question count and time limit are in the current PSI Mississippi candidate bulletin, and the passing score on MSBOC trade exams is 70%. MSBOC lets candidates use only the references named in the PSI bulletin, which is why every book package here matches that list.

Do I also take the Law and Business Management exam?

Every applicant also passes the Mississippi Law and Business Management exam: 50 questions, a 70% passing score and 2 hours, taken open book at PSI. Reciprocity can waive a trade exam, but it does not waive Law and Business.
