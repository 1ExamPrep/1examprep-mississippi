Which Mississippi exam is it for?

The Mississippi Heavy Construction license covers bridges, dams, industrial plants, large site work and the heavy civil work around them. In Mississippi, a commercial certificate of responsibility from the Mississippi State Board of Contractors is needed for commercial jobs over $50,000, including labor and materials. PSI gives the Heavy Construction trade exam open book; the question count and time limit are in the current PSI Mississippi candidate bulletin, and the passing score on MSBOC trade exams is 70%.
