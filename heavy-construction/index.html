Who needs the Mississippi Heavy Construction license?

The Heavy Construction classification covers bridges, dams, industrial plants, large site work and the heavy civil work around them. In Mississippi, a commercial certificate of responsibility from the Mississippi State Board of Contractors is needed for commercial jobs over $50,000, including labor and materials. Most buyers are civil and industrial contractors. The license goes to a business, and its qualifying party is the person who sits for the exams.

What is on the Heavy Construction exam?

PSI gives the Heavy Construction trade exam open book; the question count and time limit are in the current PSI Mississippi candidate bulletin, and the passing score on MSBOC trade exams is 70%. MSBOC lets candidates use only the references named in the PSI bulletin, which is why every book package here matches that list.

Is the Mississippi contractor exam open book?

Yes. MSBOC says every exam is given by PSI and every exam is open book, so tabbed and highlighted books save real time.

Do I also take the Law and Business Management exam?

Every applicant also passes the Mississippi Law and Business Management exam: 50 questions, a 70% passing score and 2 hours, taken open book at PSI. Reciprocity can waive a trade exam, but it does not waive Law and Business.
