Which Mississippi exam is it for?

The Mississippi Plumbing license covers commercial plumbing systems, fixtures, water distribution and drainage. In Mississippi, a commercial certificate of responsibility from the Mississippi State Board of Contractors is needed for commercial jobs over $50,000, including labor and materials. PSI gives the Plumbing trade exam open book; the question count and time limit are in the current PSI Mississippi candidate bulletin, and the passing score on MSBOC trade exams is 70%.

How should you study with it?

Spend 30 to 60 minutes a day for 4 to 6 weeks. Keep the approved books open while you practice so the course trains your lookups, not just your memory. Pair it with the Law and Business Management prep, since every applicant sits that 50-question exam too.
