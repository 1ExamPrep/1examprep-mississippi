Which Mississippi exam is it for?

The Mississippi License Application and Business Setup license is help with the MSBOC application, the business entity filing and the paperwork that comes before the exam. In Mississippi, a commercial certificate of responsibility from the Mississippi State Board of Contractors is needed for commercial jobs over $50,000, including labor and materials, and a residential license is needed for remodeling or roofing work over $10,000. PSI gives the License Application and Business Setup trade exam open book; the question count and time limit are in the current PSI Mississippi candidate bulletin, and the passing score on MSBOC trade exams is 70%.
