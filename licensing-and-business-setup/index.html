Who needs the Mississippi License Application and Business Setup license?

The License Application and Business Setup classification is help with the MSBOC application, the business entity filing and the paperwork that comes before the exam. In Mississippi, a commercial certificate of responsibility from the Mississippi State Board of Contractors is needed for commercial jobs over $50,000, including labor and materials, and a residential license is needed for remodeling or roofing work over $10,000. Most buyers are contractors who want the application done right the first time. The license goes to a business, and its qualifying party is the person who sits for the exams.

What is on the License Application and Business Setup exam?

PSI gives the License Application and Business Setup trade exam open book; the question count and time limit are in the current PSI Mississippi candidate bulletin, and the passing score on MSBOC trade exams is 70%. MSBOC lets candidates use only the references named in the PSI bulletin, which is why every book package here matches that list.

Do I also take the Law and Business Management exam?

Every applicant also passes the Mississippi Law and Business Management exam: 50 questions, a 70% passing score and 2 hours, taken open book at PSI. Reciprocity can waive a trade exam, but it does not waive Law and Business.
