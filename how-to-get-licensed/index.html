How to get a Mississippi contractor license

To get a Mississippi contractor license, choose your MSBOC classification, register the business, apply to the board, then pass 2 open-book exams at PSI: your trade exam and the 50-question Law and Business Management exam, each at 70%. Most applicants finish in 6 to 10 weeks.

Key takeaways

  • Mississippi licenses by dollar limit: commercial and new homes over $50,000, remodeling or roofing over $10,000.
  • Apply to MSBOC first; PSI emails a scheduling notice only after the board approves you.
  • Pass 2 open-book exams at PSI, your trade exam and the 50-question Law and Business exam, each at 70%.
  • Most applicants finish in 6 to 10 weeks, and paperwork causes more delays than the exams.

Do you need a state license at all?

Mississippi sets dollar limits rather than licensing every job. A commercial certificate of responsibility from the Mississippi State Board of Contractors is needed for commercial jobs over $50,000, including labor and materials. For homes, a residential license is needed to build new homes valued over $50,000, and a residential license is needed for remodeling or roofing work over $10,000. Fire sprinkler work is stricter, at $5,000 on public jobs and $10,000 on private ones.

Step 1: which classification fits your work?

MSBOC licenses by classification. A general builder usually wants Building Construction or the NASCLA Commercial General Building exam, while home builders take Residential Builder. Trades such as Mechanical, Roofing and Concrete each have their own exam. This site covers 50 license types with matching prep.

Step 2: set up the business

The license is issued to a business, so form the company first and register it with the Mississippi Secretary of State. Line up general liability and, once you have employees, workers' compensation. A business setup service can handle the filing if you would rather not.

Step 3: apply to the Mississippi State Board of Contractors

Submit the MSBOC application for your classification. Commercial applicants include financial statements with the application, and residential applicants file a shorter form. The board reviews eligibility before anything else happens, so a complete file on day 1 can save weeks.

Step 4: when can you schedule at PSI?

After the board confirms you are eligible, PSI emails a scheduling notice. MSBOC is clear that you cannot book a seat before that email arrives. Book the trade exam and Law and Business on the same day if seats allow, and give yourself at least 3 to 4 weeks of study before the date.

Step 5: how do you pass both exams?

Every exam is open book, but only the references in the PSI bulletin are allowed. The Law and Business exam has 50 questions in 2 hours, and trade exams run from 40 to 115 questions. Tabbed, highlighted books and timed practice are what move scores past 70%. Start with the Law and Business prep, then your trade page.

  • Read each book's table of contents twice
  • Take at least 2 full timed practice exams
  • Aim to find any answer in under 2 minutes

What delays a Mississippi license most often?

Most delays come from paperwork, not the exams. Applicants who plan for these 4 problems usually finish inside the 6 to 10 week window.

  1. An incomplete MSBOC application, which stops review until the missing page arrives.
  2. Waiting to book PSI until the week you feel ready, when nearby seats may already be taken.
  3. Bringing a book that is not on the PSI reference list, which the proctor will not allow.
  4. Skipping Law and Business prep, even though its 50 questions decide every application.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get a Mississippi contractor license?

Most applicants take 6 to 10 weeks from application to license, depending on board review, PSI seat availability and study time.

Do I need experience to get licensed?

MSBOC reviews each application against the classification you request. Check the current application on msboc.us for the experience and references it asks for.

Do residential and commercial applicants take the same business exam?

Yes. Every applicant also passes the Mississippi Law and Business Management exam: 50 questions, a 70% passing score and 2 hours, taken open book at PSI.

Does Mississippi have reciprocity?

MSBOC has reciprocal agreements with several state boards that can waive a trade exam. Law and Business Management is still required.

Do I need a license for a $40,000 commercial job?

Not from the state, since the commercial limit is $50,000. Local permits and business licenses still apply.

Who is the qualifying party?

The qualifying party is the person who passes the exams on behalf of the licensed business.
