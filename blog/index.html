Frequently asked questions

Who writes the posts on this blog?

Max Ace writes the posts. Each one answers the questions people search most about getting licensed in this state, and every answer is checked against the licensing board and exam vendor pages linked in its Sources list.

How often are new posts added?

New posts go up when the board changes a rule, a fee or an exam, and when readers keep asking a question the site does not answer yet. Each post shows the date it was last updated.

Are the blog answers legal advice?

No. The posts explain the licensing rules in plain words so you can plan, but the board has the final say. Always confirm details with the board before you apply or pay a fee.
