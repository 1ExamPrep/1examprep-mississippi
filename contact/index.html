What should you include in your message?

A few details let the team recommend the right package on the first reply instead of the third. Mississippi has 50 license types on this site, and the approved books differ for each one, so the classification matters most of all.

  • Your license classification, such as Building Construction or Residential Builder
  • Your PSI test date, or the month you hope to test
  • Which approved books you already own
  • Whether you still need to file the MSBOC application

Frequently asked questions

How do I contact 1 Exam Prep about a Mississippi exam?

Can you tell me which license I need?

Where do I ask about an order I already placed?

Use the form or email sales@1examprep.com with your order number, and the team will look it up.
