Who needs the Mississippi Journeyman Plumber license?

The Journeyman Plumber classification is a local license; Mississippi has no statewide journeyman or master license, so individual electricians and plumbers test with the city or county that issues the card. Contractors who bid jobs over the state limits still need an MSBOC license. Most buyers are plumbers testing for a city or county journeyman card. Is the Mississippi contractor exam open book?

Yes. MSBOC says every exam is given by PSI and every exam is open book, so tabbed and highlighted books save real time.

Do I also take the Law and Business Management exam?

Every applicant also passes the Mississippi Law and Business Management exam: 50 questions, a 70% passing score and 2 hours, taken open book at PSI. Reciprocity can waive a trade exam, but it does not waive Law and Business.
