Which Mississippi exam is it for?

The Mississippi Marine Construction license covers docks, piers, bulkheads, seawalls and dredging along the Gulf Coast and inland waterways. In Mississippi, a commercial certificate of responsibility from the Mississippi State Board of Contractors is needed for commercial jobs over $50,000, including labor and materials. PSI gives the Marine Construction trade exam open book; the question count and time limit are in the current PSI Mississippi candidate bulletin, and the passing score on MSBOC trade exams is 70%.

How should you study with it?

Order early, because the books are the exam. Tab each book on arrival, read the table of contents twice and practice finding answers with a timer. Pair it with the Law and Business Management prep, since every applicant sits that 50-question exam too.
